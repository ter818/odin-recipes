Tortang Talong Recipe

Eggy Eggplant

Ingredients

  • Eggplant - long slim purple, roughly 6 to 8 inches
  • Eggs - one per eggplant
  • Salt - half a teaspoon for the whole bowl of beaten egg.
  • Cooking oil - Start with about a tablespoon per eggplant and top it up between batches.

Directions

  1. Grill the eggplants directly over a gas flame or on a grill. Turn them occasionally until the skin is charred and the flesh becomes soft.
  2. Place the grilled eggplants on a plate and let them cool until easy to handle.
  3. Peel off the charred skin while keeping the stems attached.
  4. Arrange each eggplant on a flat surface. Gently flatten the flesh using a fork, spreading it into a wide oval shape without breaking it apart.
  5. Crack the eggs into a wide bowl. Add the salt and beat until well combined.
  6. Dip one flattened eggplant into the beaten egg. Make sure both sides are coated.
  7. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Place the coated eggplant in the pan. Spoon a little more beaten egg over any uncovered areas.
  8. Fry for 3 to 4 minutes, or until the bottom is golden brown and the egg has set. Carefully turn it over and cook the other side for another 2 to 3 minutes.
  9. Transfer the cooked tortang talong to a serving plate. Repeat the process with the remaining eggplants, adding more oil as needed.
  10. Serve with warm rice and banana ketchup.
