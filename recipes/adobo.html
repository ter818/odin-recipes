Adobo Recipe

Adobong Baboy made with Pinoy Love.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg (approx. 2 lbs) pork belly or pork shoulder (cut into 2-inch chunks)
  • ½ cup Filipino soy sauce
  • ⅓ to ½ cup white vinegar or cane vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed and minced
  • 1 cup water
  • 3 to 4 pieces dried bay leaves
  • ½ tablespoon whole black peppercorns (or 1 tsp ground pepper)
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • Sugar to taste (optional, for balancing acidity)
  • Salt or fish sauce (patis) to taste

Directions

  1. Sear the pork: Heat the cooking oil in a large pan or pot over medium-high heat. Add the pork chunks and sear until browned on all sides and the fat begins to render.
  2. Saute aromatics:Add the minced garlic and saute for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Simmer the meat: Pour in the soy sauce, water, bay leaves, and whole peppercorns. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 40 to 50 minutes until the pork becomes tender.
  4. Add the vinegar:Uncover the pot and pour in the vinegar. Do not stir for the first 3 minutes to let the vinegar cook through without a raw taste.
  5. Season and reduce: Add sugar, salt, or a splash of fish sauce to taste. Simmer uncovered until the liquid reduces into a rich, glossy sauce (or cook longer on high heat if you prefer a drier, caramelized finish).
