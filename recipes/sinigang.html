Sinigang Recipe

Maasim asim at nakakakilig na sinigang na baboy.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg baboy (pork belly, ribs, o sinigang cut)
  • 8 tasa ng tubig
  • 1 piraso sibuyas
  • 2 piraso kamatis
  • 2-3 kutsara patis
  • 1 pakete Sinigang sa Sampalok mix
  • Gabi
  • Labanos
  • Sitaw
  • Talbos ng kangkong
  • Okra
  • Siling pangsigang
  • Asin at paminta

Directions

  1. Boil the pork: In a large pot, combine water (or rice-washing), onions, and tomatoes. Bring to a boil, then add the pork. Let it boil and skim off the scum that rises to the surface to keep the broth clear.
  2. Tenderize the meat: Cover the pot, lower the heat, and let it simmer for 45 to 60 minutes (or until the pork is fully tender).
  3. Add taro and souring agent: Add the gabi (taro) and let it simmer until it starts to soften and thicken the broth. Stir in the sinigang mix (or tamarind juice) and fish sauce.
  4. Layer the vegetables: Add the labanos (radish) and sitaw (string beans) first, then simmer for a few minutes. Follow with the okra and siling pangsigang (green long chilies)
  5. Add the greens: Toss in the kangkong (water spinach), starting with the stalks, then the leaves. Turn off the heat immediately and cover the pot; the residual heat will perfectly cook the greens.
  6. Serve: Taste the broth and adjust with more fish sauce or salt if needed. Serve hot with steamed white rice and a side of fish sauce with crushed chilies for dipping.
